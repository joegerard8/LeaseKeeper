COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
Harbor Market Food Hall
Unit 7, 118 Harbor Street, Portland, OR 97201

This Lease is made between Harbor Market Properties LP ("Landlord") and
Harbor Street Kitchen, Inc. ("Tenant"), a full-service restaurant tenant,
for Unit 7 of the above property, commencing July 1, 2026.

ARTICLE 3 - RENT AND CHARGES
Section 3.1. Base rent is $5,400.00 per month, due on the 1st of each month.

ARTICLE 9 - HEALTH, SAFETY, AND LICENSING
Section 9.1. Tenant shall maintain a valid state food service establishment
permit at all times and shall renew such permit annually, with the current
permit set to expire and require renewal by August 31, 2026.

Section 9.4. Tenant shall have the kitchen's commercial grease trap cleaned
and serviced by a licensed vendor no less than once every ninety (90) days.
The first scheduled cleaning after lease commencement shall occur no later
than September 1, 2026.

Section 9.6. Tenant shall have the kitchen fire suppression system
(Ansul or equivalent) inspected and certified by a licensed contractor on a
semi-annual basis, with the first inspection due by October 1, 2026.

ARTICLE 12 - INSURANCE
Section 12.1. Tenant shall provide Landlord a certificate of insurance for
commercial general liability coverage of not less than $1,000,000 prior to
occupancy and shall renew this certificate annually thereafter. The renewal
certificate is due each year no later than July 1.

ARTICLE 16 - ALTERATIONS
Section 16.2. Tenant shall not make structural alterations to the premises
without providing Landlord thirty (30) days' advance written notice and
receiving Landlord's prior written consent.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the date
first written above.
