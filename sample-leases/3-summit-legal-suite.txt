COMMERCIAL OFFICE LEASE AGREEMENT
Summit Corporate Tower
Floor 9, Suite 905, 1200 Summit Parkway, Denver, CO 80202

This Lease is entered into between Summit Tower Ventures LLC ("Landlord")
and Calloway & Reyes LLP ("Tenant"), a professional office tenant, for
Suite 905 of the above property, commencing January 1, 2026, for an initial
term of seven (7) years.

ARTICLE 5 - RENT
Section 5.1. Tenant shall pay monthly base rent of $9,800.00, due on the
first business day of each month. Base rent shall increase by three percent
(3%) annually on each anniversary of the commencement date.

ARTICLE 10 - INSURANCE AND LIABILITY
Section 10.3. Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance
with limits of not less than $3,000,000 and shall furnish Landlord with a
certificate of insurance evidencing such coverage upon lease commencement
and upon each annual renewal thereafter. The first annual renewal
certificate is due January 1, 2027.

ARTICLE 13 - BUILDING SYSTEMS AND SAFETY
Section 13.2. Tenant shall ensure that all fire alarm and life-safety
equipment located within the premises is inspected and certified by a
licensed fire safety contractor annually, with the first inspection due no
later than February 1, 2026.

Section 13.5. Tenant shall arrange for professional deep-cleaning and filter
replacement of premises-exclusive HVAC units twice per year, with the first
service due by April 1, 2026.

ARTICLE 17 - ACCESSIBILITY COMPLIANCE
Section 17.1. Tenant shall obtain and provide to Landlord, within ninety
(90) days of the commencement date, a Certified Access Specialist (CASp)
inspection report confirming the premises' compliance with applicable
accessibility requirements.

ARTICLE 20 - NOTICES
Section 20.4. Tenant shall provide Landlord no less than one hundred eighty
(180) days' written notice prior to vacating the premises at the conclusion
of the lease term.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the date
first written above.
