COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
Riverside Commons Retail Center
Suite 210, 4400 Riverside Drive, Austin, TX 78704

This Lease is entered into between Riverside Commons Holdings LLC ("Landlord")
and Bright Smile Family Dental, PLLC ("Tenant") for the premises described
above, commencing March 1, 2026 and continuing for a term of five (5) years.

ARTICLE 4 - RENT
Section 4.1. Tenant shall pay Landlord base rent of $6,200.00 per month, due
on the first day of each calendar month, without demand, deduction, or setoff.

ARTICLE 8 - INSURANCE
Section 8.2. Tenant shall, at its own expense, maintain a commercial general
liability insurance policy with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per
occurrence, and shall deliver to Landlord a certificate of insurance
evidencing such coverage on an annual basis. The first such certificate shall
be delivered no later than March 1, 2027, and Tenant shall provide a renewed
certificate annually thereafter.

ARTICLE 11 - MAINTENANCE
Section 11.3. Tenant shall arrange, at its sole cost, for professional
servicing of all HVAC equipment serving the premises no less than once per
quarter. The first quarterly service visit shall be completed no later than
June 1, 2026.

Section 11.5. Tenant shall have the premises' fire extinguishers and
emergency lighting inspected by a licensed vendor annually, with the first
inspection due by May 15, 2026.

ARTICLE 14 - COMPLIANCE
Section 14.2. Tenant shall provide Landlord with written notice at least
sixty (60) days prior to any change in the nature of Tenant's business
operations conducted on the premises.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the date
first written above.
